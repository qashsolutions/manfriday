# Creator pilots — how we buy distribution without breaking platform rules

Written 4 Oct 2026. Rates and mechanics verified against 2026 market sources
(linked at the bottom). This replaces the vague idea of a "UGC farm" with the
version that is legal, measurable and actually used by brands.

## What we are buying

A real person, with their own face and their own account, posting a video about
Man Friday, disclosed as paid. Nothing is automated, no account is bought, and
we never post from an account we do not own. See `docs/plan_oct04.md` Phase 5
for the list of things we will never do and why.

## What $100 buys at 2026 rates

| Deliverable | Typical market rate | Fits $100? |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok video posted by a nano creator (1K–10K followers) | $20–$100 | Yes |
| Instagram Reel posted by a nano creator | $75–$200 | At the low end |
| YouTube Short posted by a nano creator | $75–$200 | At the low end |
| UGC video delivered to us, creator does not post | $75–$350, averaging ~$200 | Beginners only |
| Whitelisting / Spark Ads rights on top | adds 20–50% of the fee | Budget $120–$150 |

So the $100 pilot is realistic for TikTok-first nano creators, and tight but
possible on Reels and Shorts. Ask for Spark Ads rights at the start: adding them
later means renegotiating.

## Sourcing — our unfair advantage

Generic UGC marketplaces skew to beauty and e-commerce. Our niche is people who
build software. We already own the better tool for finding them: the same
outlier search that built the trend library.

1. **Outlier search, filtered.** Query the solo-builder niche with
   `followersMax: 5000`, last 30–90 days. That returns creators whose posts beat
   their own median — small accounts that already know how to make something land.
2. **Their own libraries.** Pull each candidate's recent posts; keep creators who
   post about building, shipping or tooling at least weekly.
3. **Marketplaces as backup**, when we need volume rather than niche fit: TikTok
   Creator Marketplace, Collabstr, JoinBrands, Billo, Influee. On those, you post
   a funded brief and creators apply, which is faster than pitching.

Screen on content quality, niche fit and reliability — not follower count.

## The pilot: 10 creators, $100 each

- **Budget:** $1,000.
- **Deliverable per creator:** one 30–60s video, posted to their own account,
  paid-partnership label on, plus the raw file and Spark Ads rights.
- **Brief:** they use Man Friday on their own product for a week and say what
  actually happened. No script from us. Honest beats polished, and the research
  shows low-production relatability outperforms.
- **Link:** every creator gets their own tracked link (`manfriday.app/l/<slug>`),
  so clicks and signups are attributed per creator. We already have the plumbing.
- **Disclosure:** paid partnership label on TikTok and Instagram, plus "#ad" in
  the caption. Non-negotiable; undisclosed paid posts breach platform rules and
  advertising law.

## How we judge it

Per creator, after 14 days: clicks to the product, signups, paid conversions.
Then the ladder:

- **Kill** anything below 20 clicks.
- **Repeat** at the same $100 for 20–100 clicks.
- **Raise** to $200–$300 a video, or a monthly retainer, above 100 clicks or any
  paid conversion.
- **Amplify** the winners with Spark Ads, since the creative is already proven.

## The payback maths, stated honestly

At $20/month Solo, a $1,000 pilot takes 50 subscriber-months to repay. That is
too slow to judge by. Judge it against the offers with real up-front value:

- Founding 200 at $200 once → **5 sales repay the pilot**.
- Annual Solo at $150 → 7 sales.
- Monthly Solo at $20 → treat as a long-term bet, not pilot payback.

This is also the argument for paying creators out of the Founding offer rather
than a share of a $20 subscription, where 30% is $6.

## Outreach template (direct, for creators we source ourselves)

> Hey [name] — I watched your [specific post] about [specific thing]. I build Man
> Friday: you paste your product's URL and it drafts short-form videos for it,
> then posts the ones you approve to TikTok and YouTube.
>
> I'd like to pay you $100 to use it on [their product] for a week and post one
> honest video about what happened — good or bad, your words, your face, your
> account. Paid-partnership label on, and I'd want the raw file plus Spark Ads
> rights so I can boost it.
>
> If it works for both of us I'd like to make it monthly. Interested?

Keep it one message, name the money, name the deliverable, and never ask for a
positive review.
