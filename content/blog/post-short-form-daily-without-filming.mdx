<Takeaways>
  <li>Filming, cutting and captioning is mechanical. The only part that needs you is judgement: keep or skip.</li>
  <li>Short-form is a volume game. The recommendation model needs dozens of posts before it knows who to show you to.</li>
  <li>So move yourself to the swipe, and let code do the rest.</li>
</Takeaways>
