<Takeaways>
  <li>You don't sell attention. A video can only do one thing for you: send someone to a page where they can buy.</li>
  <li>Every post gets its own short link in the caption. One tap, one logged click, then straight to your site with UTM tags.</li>
  <li>Link-preview crawlers are filtered out. What is left is intent.</li>
