<Takeaways>
  <li>Nobody in Mumbai pitches a product in textbook Hindi. They mix: English nouns for the product world, Hindi for anything with feeling in it.</li>
  <li>A machine translation of your English script sounds foreign within a syllable. A rewrite in that register does not.</li>
  <li>Voice, captions and script are all native to the market, including the conjuncts most renderers get wrong.</li>
</Takeaways>
